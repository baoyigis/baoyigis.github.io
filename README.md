# Yi Bao's Academic Website

Welcome to my academic homepage! This website showcases my research in urban spatiotemporal big data mining and sustainable development.

## About Me

I am **Yi Bao (鲍毅)**, an Assistant Professor at [Nanjing University](https://www.nju.edu.cn/) in the Institute of Space Earth Science. My research focuses on:

- **Urban Spatiotemporal Big Data Mining**: Revealing patterns and trends of urban development through big data analytics
- **High-Resolution Built Environment**: Exploring interaction mechanisms between built environment and human activities
- **Geographic Intelligence (GeoAI)**: Applying large language models (LLM) and GeoAgent to multimodal geographic information processing
